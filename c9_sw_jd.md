# Job Title: Lead Software Engineer
## Short Description of the Job
Apply widely agreed software engineering principles and methodologies to design, develop, test, and maintain applications and services to achieve stated business and technology goals within required budgets and timelines.

## Key Responsibilities
- Align to the organization’s architectural direction and principles, ensuring that applications and services being built adhere to them.
- Liaise with lead engineers, architects, business analysts, and key stakeholders to understand objectives and requirements.
- Drive and deliver well-thought-out estimates after thoroughly considering design, build, and testing aspects of complexity, maintainability, and serviceability.
- Design and develop high-volume, high-performance, and high-availability applications using proven frameworks and technologies.

## Join us as a Software Engineer
This is an opportunity for a technically-minded individual to join our diverse and inclusive team. You’ll be able to put your existing program management skills to the test and benefit from our development opportunities. You’ll have a tangible impact on our function and be able to advance your career.
### What You'll Do
You will apply widely agreed software engineering principles and methodologies to design, develop, test, and maintain applications and services to achieve business and technology goals within required budgets and timelines. You will also oversee the quality of work to ensure it meets technical standards for all service outputs.

Additionally, you will:
- Drive the design and development of reusable libraries and APIs for use across the organization.
- Design and develop software that supports greater automation of build, release, testing, and deployment processes in all environments.
- Encourage reuse and sharing of platform components and technologies within software engineering teams.
- Deliver software components to enable the delivery of platforms, applications, and services.
- Write unit and integration tests in automated test environments to ensure code quality.
## The Skills You'll Need
You should have:
- A strong background in software engineering, software design, or database design and architecture.
- Extensive experience developing software in a Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA) or microservices paradigm.
- Experience in programming best practices, using industry-recognized frameworks and development tooling.
- A background in implementing programming best practices, particularly in scalability, availability, and performance.
### Additional Skills:
- Experience with test-driven development (TDD) and the use of automated test frameworks, mocking, and stubbing tools.
- Knowledge of the software development lifecycle and ability to deliver software to specified timescales.
- Experience working in an environment where business requirements evolve.
- An understanding of how to troubleshoot, support, modify, and maintain systems developed by other engineering teams.
- Ability to understand and support modern engineering practices.
